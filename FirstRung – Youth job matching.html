How matching works

Each job gets a score out of 100:

50 points – required skills you have. Related skills count part-way, so “Sales” helps with “Customer service”.
15 points – nice-to-have skills.
15 points – location, or remote work.
10 points – education level.
10 points – job type you asked for.

Every score comes with reasons and the skills you are missing, so you can see what to build next. This demo keeps data in your browser session only. To use it for real, connect the job list to a database and replace the skill-similarity table with text embeddings.
